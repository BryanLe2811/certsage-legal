Delete your account and data

Last updated: 15 September 2026

You can delete your CertSage account and everything associated with it, at any time, for any reason — from inside the app, or by emailing us if you have already uninstalled it. This page explains both routes, what gets removed, and what is kept.

How to delete your account

There are two routes. The first is immediate and does not involve us at all.

In the app

Profile tab → scroll to the bottom → Delete account.

You are shown exactly what is about to be removed and asked to type DELETE to confirm. It takes effect the moment you confirm — your data is already gone by the time you are signed back out. Nothing is queued, and nobody reviews it.

By email

If you have already uninstalled the app, email us from the address on your account:

support.certsage@integolutions.com

Subject line: Delete my account

Sending from your account's own email address is what lets us verify the request is really yours. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use that same address. If you can no longer access it, write to us anyway and we will find another way to verify you before deleting anything.

How long it takes

In the app — immediately. The deletion runs as you confirm it. There is no review step, no waiting period, and no way for us to reverse it afterwards.

By email — within 30 days. We reply within 7 days to confirm the request came from you, then carry out the deletion and confirm in writing once it is done.

If you have an active subscription, cancel it in Google Play (or the App Store) before deleting your account — we cannot cancel a store subscription on your behalf, and deleting the account does not stop billing by itself. See below.

What gets deleted

Everything that identifies you or records what you did in the app:

  • Your login credentials and authentication records.
  • Your profile — email address, name, occupation, time zone, notification preferences.
  • Every certification enrolment, along with your goals, experience level, target exam date and the free-text motivation you wrote.
  • Your full study history: sessions, mastery per concept, readiness scores, streaks.
  • Every message you exchanged with the AI coach.
  • Every quiz question you were asked, the answers you gave and whether they were correct.
  • All practice exam attempts and per-question results.
  • Your cached subscription status and entitlements.

These are removed together, in one cascading operation. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — there is no recovery window and no soft-delete. If you want a copy of your data, ask for an export before requesting deletion.

What is kept, and why

Four categories of record survive. For the first three, the link to your account is removed. The fourth is different and we would rather say so than bury it: the analytics record is left as it is and ages out on its own. We describe each precisely rather than calling them all anonymous, because two of them are not quite:

RecordWhat happens to itWhy
AI usage and cost logs The reference to your account is set to null. What remains is a model name, token counts, a cost and a timestamp — with no way to attribute it to you. Cost history has to stay accurate after accounts close, or the service cannot be costed at all.
Billing event logs The reference to your account is set to null. The stored copy of the original event from our subscription provider still contains the internal identifier your account used, so these are pseudonymous rather than anonymous — we keep the event exactly as it arrived. Required for tax, accounting and resolving payment disputes, which can only be reconstructed from the original event.
Crash and performance reports Reports created before you deleted your account stay with our diagnostics provider (Sentry) until their 90-day retention expires, then delete automatically. They are keyed to the same internal identifier, so these are pseudonymous too. Ask us and we will delete them sooner. They exist to diagnose faults. We cannot fix a crash we can no longer see, and the window is short and self-clearing.
Product analytics records Nothing is set to null here. Deleting your account stops further activity being attributed to you, but the record already held by our analytics provider (PostHog) stays as it is until its 1-year retention expires, then deletes automatically. It is keyed to the same internal identifier, so it is pseudonymous too. Ask us and we will delete it sooner. Knowing which features people use, and where they get stuck, is how the app gets better. It holds no message, quiz or article text.

None of these contain your email address, your name, your messages, or the text of any quiz question, answer or article. The analytics record is the one that holds anything about your studying, and only as figures: that a session started or finished, that an answer was right or wrong, an exam score, a readiness percentage. What the last three retain is an internal identifier — the same one your account used — with nothing left that maps it back to a person.

Separately, our providers keep their own records under their own policies and retention schedules — Google Play for purchases, and RevenueCat for subscription state. We can ask them to delete on your behalf but cannot do it directly.

If you have a paid subscription

Deleting your CertSage account does not cancel your subscription and does not issue a refund. Store subscriptions are managed entirely by the store, not by us:

  • Android — Google Play app → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CertSage → Cancel.
  • iOS — Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CertSage → Cancel.

Refunds are handled by the store under its own policy. Cancel first, confirm the cancellation has taken effect, then request deletion.

If you do not want to delete everything

  • Just want to stop? Sign out. Your account stays intact and nothing further is processed until you sign back in.
  • Want to correct something? Most profile and goal fields are editable in Settings. You do not need us for that.
  • Want a copy of your data? Email the same address and ask for an export. We provide it in a machine-readable format within 30 days.
  • Want only part of it removed — say, your chat transcripts but not your progress? Ask. We will tell you honestly whether it is possible.
