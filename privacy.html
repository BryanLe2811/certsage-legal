3. What we collect

a. Account and profile

  • Email address — required to create an account and to sign you in.
  • Password — only if you sign up with email and password. It is stored by our authentication provider as a salted hash. It is never visible to us and cannot be recovered, only reset.
  • Google or Apple sign-in identity — if you choose that route, we receive the account identifier, your email address, and the display name and profile picture URL those services choose to return. We do not receive your password.
  • Preferred name — what you would like the app to call you. Asked once, after sign-up.
  • Occupation — chosen from a fixed list of ten options, one of which is "Prefer not to say". There is no free-text box, deliberately.
  • Time zone — the IANA identifier reported by your device (for example Australia/Brisbane), so daily usage limits reset at your local midnight rather than in UTC.
  • Notification preferences.

b. Your study goals

Asked once per certification you take on, and editable at any time in Settings:

  • Your self-rated experience level with that technology.
  • Your reason for studying, chosen from a fixed list.
  • A free-text description of your motivation, in your own words — optional. Please do not put sensitive personal information in this field; it is sent to our AI provider as context for the coach.
  • Your target exam date and weekly study goal.

c. Learning activity

  • Which certification tracks you are enrolled in, which is currently active, and a record of each switch between them — including the reason you gave and your readiness score at that moment.
  • Each study session: its mode (Interactive, Listening or Reading), when it started and ended, and which topic it covered.
  • The messages you send to the AI coach and the replies it gives, stored as a transcript so the coach has continuity within a session.
  • Every quiz question shown to you, the answer you gave, whether it was correct, and the resulting mastery score for that concept.
  • Practice exam attempts and per-question results.
  • Derived progress data: readiness percentages per domain and overall, study streaks, session counts and average scores.

d. Subscription and payment

  • We never receive, process or store payment card details. Purchases are processed by Google Play (or the App Store on iOS) under their own terms.
  • Through our subscription manager, RevenueCat, we receive and store: a pseudonymous customer identifier (your account's internal UUID), your plan tier, the platform you purchased on, subscription status, renewal date and auto-renew flag.
  • We keep a verbatim copy of the subscription events sent to us, so a billing dispute can be reconstructed exactly as it happened.

e. Operational records

  • For each AI call made on your behalf: the model used, token counts, the resulting cost, the type of task and a timestamp. This exists to keep the service affordable and to enforce fair-use limits — not to profile you.
  • Standard server logs kept by our hosting provider, which include IP addresses and request timestamps, used for security, abuse prevention and debugging.
  • Crash, error and performance diagnostics, collected through Sentry. When the app or one of our server functions fails, a report is created so we can fix it without needing you to reproduce the problem. A report contains: your account identifier (the same internal ID described in 3a — not your email or your name), which subscription plan you are on, the screens you visited before the failure, which of our own server functions was called and the HTTP status it answered with, your device model, operating-system version and app version, the technical name of the error and how long each step took. Performance traces of ordinary, successful requests are collected in the same way.

    It never receives the content of anything you wrote or read — not your coaching messages, not quiz questions or answers, not your study motivation, not your name and not your email address. Where a failure involves a piece of text, we deliberately send only its length and never the text itself. We have also disabled IP-address storage on our Sentry account, so your IP is not retained there.

4. What we do not collect

Stated explicitly because the absence is a design decision, not an oversight:

  • No advertising identifier, no ad networks, no ad profiling, no cross-app tracking.
  • No location data of any kind, precise or approximate.
  • No contacts, photos, files, calendar or SMS access.
  • No microphone or camera access. The Android app does not request the RECORD_AUDIO or CAMERA permission. Voice input is planned for a future version; this policy will be updated before it ships.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDK is embedded in the app. Nothing records which screens you browse in order to profile you or to measure a marketing campaign. One diagnostic SDK is embedded — Sentry, which reports crashes, errors and performance timings so we can fix faults without having to ask you to reproduce them. What it receives is described in section 3e and section 7; it is never sent your messages, your quiz answers, your name or your email address.
  • No push notification tokens are currently collected. Push is not active in this version.
  • No health, financial, biometric, racial, religious, political or sexual-orientation data. We have no field for it and do not want it.

The Android app requests only these permissions: internet access, and — for playing Listening-mode audio with the screen off — wake lock, foreground service, media playback foreground service, and notification display. None of them read personal data from your device.

5. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account, and to sign you in.
  • To deliver the core product: generate coaching replies, quiz questions and explanations, track what you have mastered, and calculate your readiness score.
  • To choose what to recommend you study next.
  • To apply your subscription entitlements and enforce free-tier usage limits fairly.
  • To keep the service running and affordable — monitoring AI cost per user, diagnosing failures, and preventing abuse.
  • To respond when you contact us.

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not use your study data for any purpose other than running the app you are using it in.

6. How AI processing works

CertSage is built around an AI coach, so this section matters more than most. We use third-party AI providers, and we tell you precisely what reaches them.

Sent to Anthropic (Claude)

When you talk to the coach, take a quiz, or open the home screen, we send:

  • The messages you type to the coach, and the conversation so far in that session.
  • The names of the certification, domain, sub-topic and concepts you are studying.
  • Your progress state — which concepts you are weak on, improving on, or have not seen.
  • Your study goals, including the free-text motivation you wrote, your experience level and your target exam date.
  • Your first name, used only to open the home-screen greeting.
  • Short summaries of your recent sessions, for continuity.

We do not send your email address, your password, any payment information, or any device identifier.

Sent to OpenAI (text-to-speech only)

Listening-mode audio is generated in advance from narration scripts that we write and review, and then cached and served to every learner. No personal data of any kind is sent to OpenAI — it never receives your messages, your progress or your identity. We use no OpenAI text model.

Training, retention and human review

  • Anthropic and OpenAI both state that data submitted through their commercial APIs is not used to train their models. We have not opted into any programme that would change that.
  • Those providers may retain API inputs and outputs for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring, under their own policies, which are linked in section 7.
  • We may read stored session transcripts when investigating a specific fault or a report of abuse. We do not read them routinely.

Automated decision-making

Your readiness score and the topics recommended to you are generated automatically. These affect only what the app suggests you study. They produce no legal effect and no similarly significant effect on you within the meaning of GDPR Article 22, and nothing about your account, billing or access depends on them.

Please do not type sensitive personal information into the coach. Chat messages are stored against your account and sent to our AI provider to produce a reply. Treat the coach as you would a study group, not a private diary.

Crash, error and performance diagnostics (Sentry) A rolling 90 days, after which Sentry deletes them automatically. These are keyed to your account identifier, so deleting your account does not erase reports created before it — see the note below.

The exceptions, stated plainly. Deleting your account removes everything that identifies you and everything you wrote. Three kinds of record are not destroyed by it, and we would rather describe them precisely than claim more than is true.

AI cost records. The reference to your account is set to null, leaving a model name, token counts, a cost and a timestamp with nothing in the row that points back to you. We keep them because accurate cost history has to outlive the accounts it came from.

Raw billing event logs. The account reference is likewise set to null, but the stored copy of the original event from our subscription provider still contains the internal identifier your account used. Removing the link makes these records pseudonymous rather than anonymous: they hold no email address, no name and nothing you wrote, but we will not claim the identifier is gone when it is not. We keep them verbatim because a payment dispute can only be reconstructed from the original event.

Crash and performance diagnostics. Reports created before you deleted your account remain with Sentry until their 90-day retention expires, and they are keyed to the same account identifier — pseudonymous, for the same reason as above. They contain no message content, no quiz content, no name and no email address. If you would like them removed before that window expires, email us and we will delete them.

13. How we protect your data

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS. The Android build blocks unencrypted connections outright.
  • Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider.
  • Every table enforces row-level security: a request carrying your credentials can only ever read rows belonging to you, enforced by the database itself rather than by app code.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed by our authentication provider. We never see them.
  • Graded results and quiz answer keys are writable only by our server, never by the app, so results cannot be forged and answers cannot be read ahead of time.
  • Access to production data is limited to the developer named in section 1.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority as required by law.

14. Children

CertSage is intended for adults pursuing professional technology certifications. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it. Where local law sets a lower age of digital consent, that lower age applies.
